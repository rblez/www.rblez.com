01About

I build software that solves real problems for real people in Cuba.

I’m a software engineer in Santiago de Cuba. I design and build complete products end to end: the database, the payments, the notifications and the interface people use every day. Right now I’m building Tiendly.

Tiendly is a platform to create online stores with 0% commission, made for Cuban sellers. Every store gets its own catalog, cart and link, and the seller gets each order instantly on Telegram or WhatsApp.

Selling online in Cuba is unlike anywhere else, so payments follow how people actually pay here: bank transfers with BANDEC, BPA and BANMET, MiTransfer, Saldo Móvil, QvaPay, Zelle, PayPal, USDT or cash on delivery.

Outside Tiendly I build bots and automations that run on their own: alerts, monitors and small services that save hours. I also make open tools, like an explorer of Cuban bank cards or an image to ASCII art converter.

Lately I’m experimenting with open AI agents that run on your own key. I care about simple: fewer screens, fewer steps and products you understand without a manual.

I'm an e-commerce lover: I'm obsessed with someone posting a product and a stranger buying it miles away, without ever seeing it in person. In Cuba that's still hard — payments, shipping, trust — so I built Tiendly: to make selling online as simple as posting a photo.
